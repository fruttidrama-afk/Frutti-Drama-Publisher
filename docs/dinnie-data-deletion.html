Dinnie Publisher Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 25, 2026

If you connected a Facebook account to Dinnie Publisher and want the application's stored Facebook authorization data removed, disconnect the Facebook integration from Dinnie Publisher and revoke the app from your Facebook account's Apps and Websites settings.

After revocation, the application can no longer use the revoked authorization. If you need additional assistance, contact the application owner through the account associated with the Dinnie The Dinosaur Page.
